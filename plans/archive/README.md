# Completed plan archive

Files under `archive/completed/` are closed, historical planning records. They
do not dispatch work and must not be used to reopen an execution unit.

Current plan status and the sole executable checkpoint remain in
[`plans/README.md`](../README.md), especially its **Active dispatch** block.
Historical tracker rows and `plans/evidence/001/**` remain in their original
locations because current validation and provenance replay consume them.

## Completed plans

- [Plan 001 — Build the Salt AI knowledge platform release candidate](./completed/001-build-salt-ai-knowledge-platform.md)
- [Plan 001a — Reuse the CLI and MCP names after unused test snapshots](./completed/001a-reuse-test-snapshot-package-identities.md)

Deferred, proposed, or partly completed plans are intentionally not archived.
Git paths quoted by completed evidence or replay validators remain historical
identifiers and are not rewritten to their new archive location.
