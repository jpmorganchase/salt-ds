Sol identified the saved email's missing suffix in the 320-pixel image and
connected it to the definition-list grid's `max-content 1fr` sizing. It also
correctly observed that the acceptance check's text-presence and document-width
assertions did not establish that the complete email was visibly readable.

Sol noted missing justification for custom primary navigation, but did not
establish the matching Salt component or report replacement of the custom
navigation as an actionable finding. This does not satisfy the second frozen
discrimination. A replay of its actual query, `application primary navigation
current destination overview team settings browser history`, returned
BrowserIcon, BrowserSolidIcon, HistoryIcon, SettingsIcon and SettingsSolidIcon;
it returned no navigation guidance. The current bundle therefore also exposed a
concrete task-retrieval weakness. This observation does not isolate the cause of
the review miss or justify a general model-quality claim.

In the expected-pass case, Sol found that Show/Hide review details still used
a Salt Button with manually controlled conditional content. Independent
read-only diagnosis confirmed the finding against the unchanged Skill's
Salt-coverage requirement, the installed `start-with-intent` primitive chooser,
`record:component:component.collapsible`, and its complete controlled example.
CollapsibleTrigger can contain the existing Button with its changing label, and
CollapsiblePanel can contain the saved-snapshot definition list. There was no
task-specific gap requiring a custom disclosure.

This is a component-selection finding, not a demonstrated runtime or
accessibility failure. It does not prohibit the disclosure action inside
DialogContent, require removing the definition list, or create a universal
composition rule. The supervised correction had fixed the two previously known
issues but had not established complete Salt coverage. Its expected approval was
invalidated as required by the frozen protocol; neither source nor scoring key
was rewritten to turn an initial miss into success.
