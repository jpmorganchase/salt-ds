# Adjudication protocol

Two reviewers who cannot see delivery mode independently atomize every
assessable Salt-specific claim in prose, rationale, and code. Each claim is
`supported`, `unsupported`, or `not_assessable` against manifest-selected source
evidence. Reviewers reconcile only after their initial labels are sealed;
remaining disagreements go to the named independent reviewer. The sanitized
receipt records numerators, denominators, agreement, adjudication IDs, and
intervals, never claim text or model output.

Empty or nonresponsive output fails task success. An underpowered mode cannot
claim the unsupported-claim-rate gate. Reviewers do not change rubrics,
thresholds, allowed variants, or evidence after seeing mode outcomes.
