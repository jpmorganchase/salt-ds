Layering helps establish visual hierarchy so users can focus on the content and
data relevant to their task. Background contrast can make content regions more
prominent while keeping the surrounding application interface visually quieter.

## Backgrounds

A common arrangement places primary content containers, such as cards and
panels, on a secondary application background. The difference in fill separates
the content regions from their surroundings.

| Region in this arrangement | Background | Token                                   |
| -------------------------- | ---------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Application background     | Secondary  | `--salt-container-secondary-background` |
| Content containers         | Primary    | `--salt-container-primary-background`   |

Choose backgrounds to suit the layout and content. An interface can also use
primary throughout. If a region needs a visible boundary against a matching
background, use a border or elevation to distinguish it.

Tertiary is used less often, to highlight key content areas. See the
[container characteristic](/salt/themes/design-tokens/container-characteristic)
for each background's uses.

Background variant and elevation are independent choices. Containers can be
flat or raised, depending on the prominence their content needs.

## Flat containers

Flat containers suit data-dense interfaces, where users scan and compare
information with minimal visual noise. In this example, primary cards stand
apart from the secondary background without a shadow.

`Panel` is flat by default. Set `elevation="flat"` on `Card` to remove its
shadow.

## Raised containers

Raised containers use a shadow to give content more prominence and separation
from its surroundings. `Card` is raised by default; set `elevation="raised"`
on `Panel`. See [Card elevation](/salt/components/card/examples#elevation).

For a container of your own, use the region shadow,
`--salt-overlayable-shadow-region`; see
[shadow](/salt/foundations/elevation/index) for the shadow ramp.
