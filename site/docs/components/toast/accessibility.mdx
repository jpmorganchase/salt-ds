## Best practices

`Toast` defaults to `role="alert"`. For routine, nonurgent updates, override this with `role="status"` to request a polite announcement. Its visual `status` does not change the announcement role or determine urgency. Describe the outcome in text rather than relying on color or the status icon.

Avoid announcing the same message through both a toast and another live region. Test announcements with the browsers and screen readers you support; an announcement role does not move focus to the toast or its actions.

Check keyboard access to every action and dismiss control. Keep actionable toasts available while the user reads or interacts with them, and ensure dismissal leaves a sensible focus position. The host application owns timing and dismissal; follow the [persistence and dismissal guidance](./usage#persistence-and-dismissal) and keep essential information and recovery actions accessible elsewhere.
