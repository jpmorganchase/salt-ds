## With icon

Include a leading or trailing icon to reinforce the destination or direction of travel. Most often, place the icon in the trailing position—such as an arrow-right or the automatic new-tab icon—to reinforce moving forward or leaving the current context. Use a leading icon, such as an arrow-left, when it adds affordance for moving back to a previous page or step.
