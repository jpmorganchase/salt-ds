Added `@deprecated` tags to props that were deprecated only in their descriptions, so that editors strike them through: `Card`'s `disabled` and `interactable`, `Checkbox`'s and `RadioButton`'s `error`, and `FlexLayout`'s `separators`. Added the version to the deprecation of `AriaAnnounce`'s `delay`, and moved the deprecation of `Dialog`'s `idProp` after its description.
