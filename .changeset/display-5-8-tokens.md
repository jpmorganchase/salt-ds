Added font size and line height tokens for the new Display 5 to Display 8 text styles to all themes and densities: `--salt-text-display5-fontSize` to `--salt-text-display8-fontSize` and `--salt-text-display5-lineHeight` to `--salt-text-display8-lineHeight`. They share the existing `--salt-text-display-*` font family, font weight, letter spacing, text transform and font style tokens.
