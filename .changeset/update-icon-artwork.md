Updated Salt symbols on a 16×16 canvas, preserving icon meanings and public names. Public names and meanings are unchanged.

Stabilized retained features between outline and solid variants in Handshake, Picnic, Layers and Type. Balanced filled scatter points and candle bodies with their outline contours, used flat inverse line endings in Document and FolderClosed, and lightened CurrencyExchange’s secondary arrows.
